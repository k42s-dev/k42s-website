Projects

Most engagements start as a fixed-price project — a CI/CD pipeline built from scratch, a Terraform migration, an automation cleanup. You get a defined scope and a fixed number before any work starts, so there's no ambiguity about what the invoice looks like at the end. This works best for anything with a clear "done" state.

Retainers

For the ongoing, lighter-touch support that doesn't have a natural endpoint, k42s runs on a simple hourly retainer — 4 hours a week, billed monthly, cancel any time. No meetings overload, no on-call, no long-term contract. You get consistent, predictable help exactly when you need it, without the overhead of a fixed-scope quote for work that's inherently open-ended.
