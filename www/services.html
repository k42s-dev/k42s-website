Services

k42s handles CI/CD pipelines across GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, and Azure DevOps, and infrastructure automation with Ansible and Terraform — the kind of work that stops deployments from being a manual ritual and keeps pipelines from breaking in the first place. Whether it's a fixed-price project or a few focused hours a week, the work gets scoped, done, and handed off — documented well enough that your team can run with it after k42s is gone.

Kubernetes work is also on offer, currently scoped to smaller, well-defined engagements while k42s builds out its certification track — so if your cluster needs cleaning up or hardening, get in touch and we'll figure out together whether it's the right fit yet. Either way, it's engineering support without the overhead of a hiring process, a full-time seat, or a meeting-heavy retainer — see the Pricing page for how that actually breaks down.
