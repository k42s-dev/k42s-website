About

k42s is one person for now: an infrastructure engineer with almost three decades of hands-on experience, currently expanding that with Kubernetes, CI/CD, and automation. It started with Linux systems in the late '90s, moved through infrastructure consulting, systems engineering, and enterprise software work, and picked up a stack of certifications along the way — RHCE, LPIC-1, and a Scrum Master badge to name some.

k42s exists because "hire another full-time engineer" is often the wrong answer to "our Kubernetes/CI/CD setup needs work" — most teams need a few focused hours a week, not a new headcount. That's the whole model: scoped projects or a light weekly retainer, no on-call, no meeting overload, no long hiring process.

The name is a nod to two things at once — the k8s-style numeronym convention, and the number that, per a certain guide to the galaxy, answers everything. Draw your own conclusions about whether Kubernetes qualifies.
